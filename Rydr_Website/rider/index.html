RydrBank

See what you’ve earned.

Complete 10 eligible rides of at least 5 miles in the same reward group to earn a matching RydrBank code. A reward can cover up to 15 miles. Check eligible offers and apply promo codes before requesting a ride. Rydr funds rider discounts so your driver’s pay stays the same.

What you’ll find in RydrBank

  • Progress toward your next reward, grouped by ride type
  • Banked rides and eligible redemption options
  • Promotions and promo codes
